## Feature-specific acceptance

Enable only the features needed for the chosen deployment; leave the others disabled or explicitly unaccepted.

- **Encrypted foreign USB:** physical password/key unlock, safe lock/eject, reconnect and failed-password tests. LUKS/ext4 and LUKS/NTFS file-backed workflows passed; software-encrypted APFS passed a known-file fixture. BitLocker/To Go crypto vectors matched known decrypted hashes, but their NTFS metadata did not mount; complete BitLocker browsing still needs a clean filesystem test. No universal-format or hardware-bound Mac encryption claim.
- **Media/DLNA:** actual chosen Apple TV/Roku/Fire TV/TV players, approved-client playback and revoked/unapproved denial, scanner/scraper error handling, and ARMhf resource/codec limits. Browser-native playback is not a transcoding service.
- **VPN/private LAN:** actual provider/client and physical LAN client tests, reboot/tunnel-loss kill switches, DNS leak checks, management continuity, firewall rules and DHCP separation. Private LAN is currently disabled. Namespace tests are useful evidence, not physical gateway commissioning.
- **Downloads/torrents:** isolated HTTP process-restart resume and a local 2 MiB torrent payload passed. A full destination can damage resume metadata; the deployed manager preserves/flags the job and avoids blind retries. Automatic damaged-marker repair, external intended peers, NAS reboot interruption and broader capacity/resource acceptance remain open. See [unattended checks](UNATTENDED-VALIDATION-2026-11-01.md).
- **Cooling/watchdog:** the high/full adaptive profile, independent guard and three diskless-RAM watchdog expiry resets passed. PID-1 feeding and automatic startup passed; no expiry was induced with mounted storage. This does not accept arbitrary fan levels, electrical stall or thermal-trip behavior.
- **10GbE, SuperSpeed and wake:** separate physical acceptance remains required. Bounded MRRS-workaround USB writes and later repeated TR-004 reads do not establish sustained mounted enclosure acceptance.

## Private use versus public release

A private installed NAS need not wait for a polished public installer once its adopted functions pass the operational gates. A public turnkey release additionally needs a clean pinned build, complete dependency/corresponding-source/license inventory, signed immutable artifacts, interruption-safe guided install/recovery and independent repeat installation acceptance. The owner-approved source and showcase are published under the personal mastechnica/FreeQNAP repository; this does not clear binary-installer or physical acceptance gates.

Full-chip/SPI bootloader restoration remains **excluded by owner decision**, not silently accepted or added back to this plan. Retain verified private original backups and the tested UART/TFTP RAM rescue path, with the residual bootloader-failure limitation stated clearly.

With initial synchronization and logical same-member recovery complete, finish the attended hardened-kernel rollback/cold-start checks and post-write shutdown/data-array checks.
