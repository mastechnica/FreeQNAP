# Personal audio cards — 4 November 2026

Music, Books, Internet Radio and podcast-show cards have a separate three-dot metadata/cover editor. Podcast expansion and playback targets remain separate. Book/music edits use the existing native sidecar workflow and optional manual metadata lookup. Podcast/radio edits are account-scoped display overrides, including cover art, title, author/publisher, genres, synopsis and website; they never rewrite feed/stream destinations. Edited show/station metadata remains searchable. Shared collections remain read-only.

Raster cover uploads are limited to 1 MiB. Authorization is repeated before cached artwork or conditional responses, account keys are derived from the current session, and source/owner overrides are rejected. SVG/HTML covers are rejected. Uploaded art is decoded by the browser; no privileged server image decoder is added for show/station edits. Book/music art retains its isolated native decoder.

AAX originals can be retained as audiobooks with embedded metadata and covers. They are explicitly marked original-only, with compatible authorized-player guidance and an optional DRM-free companion. Importing does not remove DRM or prove browser playback. Subject genre is separate from audiobook classification. Native upload/scan acceptance and retained podcast pagination are staged until a safe storage-worker reload; the web update does not interrupt active copies/imports.
