## Feed compatibility

The reader normally retains its small-feed limit. Oversized feeds use a streaming fallback bounded to 16 MiB of input, 64 XML levels and up to 300 feed-order episodes; the canonical metadata snapshot remains at most 2 MiB. Entity/DOCTYPE declarations are rejected, and public-address pinning, redirect checks and TLS verification remain intact. Episode audio is not downloaded by subscription. Older archive episodes may be absent from a provider feed or the bounded listing.

The live subscription import can use the updated reader in an isolated maintenance process while copy workers stay on their immutable installed release. An already-running legacy broker still uses its older reader until a safe idle reload; manual or automatic refresh of oversized feeds can therefore still hit that legacy limit. Do not restart active file/photo imports just to reload this improvement.
