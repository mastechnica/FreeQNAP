## US/Canada discovery and book voice follow-up — 4 November 2026

The regional interface is locally deployed. Internet Radio searches the saved
list and can query the online directory by station name, genre and United States
or Canada. The reviewed starter file contains 55 sampled stations (26 US, 29 CA);
the live database retains 57 accepted regional entries including prior records.
Foreign and unclassified station records are retained privately but excluded from
listing, playback-source access and checks. Region metadata is directory data,
not a claim about the location of every streaming CDN.

Podcast search covers subscribed feeds and episodes. New subscriptions require
an explicit US/Canada service country; older unknown subscriptions remain stored
and can be classified in the interface. This is owner-declared service/publisher
country, not geographic network blocking or an online podcast directory.

The book reader offers installed local voices, reading speed and pitch from
0.5 to 2, with choices saved per browser/device. Automated speech-engine tests
verified that the selected voice, rate and pitch reach the utterance and that
remote voices are excluded. Changing settings while paused does not start audio.
Devices with no installed local voice cannot offer a voice; some speech engines
ignore pitch. Actual audible quality and intended phone/PWA behavior still need
owner listening tests. No audio was played during this campaign.
