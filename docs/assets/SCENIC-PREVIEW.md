# Illustrative scenic photo thumbnails

The owner requested sunsets and daytime beaches for the public Photos screenshot.
`scenic-thumbnails.png` is a generated contact sheet created for that purpose with
the built-in image generation tool. No personal photographs, photo-library capture,
credentials or private metadata were supplied to the generator.

The **Photos gallery** image uses a real application capture. Only its photo
thumbnail areas are replaced with crops of the scenic sheet. Controls, spacing,
colors, counters and surrounding application pixels are retained, with no claim
that the scenic photos exist in the owner's live library. It is labeled
illustrative in the showcase. Other screenshots remain unmodified live captures
apart from the documented privacy crops and network-address mask.

The NAS photo archive is unchanged. Private original screenshots and placement
verification stay local; the published receipts specify the replaced regions and
verify unchanged pixels outside those regions.

## Generation prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: one scenic photographic contact
sheet for privacy-safe example thumbnails in a family NAS photo-library screenshot.
Create a single 16:9 landscape image containing exactly 12 equal rectangular
photos, arranged 4 columns by 3 rows, aligned perfectly edge to edge with no
gutters or borders. Each individual tile should look like a beautiful real travel
photograph, and each tile is 4:3 landscape. Alternate six dramatic but natural
ocean/coast sunsets (golden sun over calm water, coral-pink sky over sea cliffs,
amber light on a rocky shore, violet twilight over a tropical bay, sunset
silhouette of palm trees, orange reflections on wet sand) with six sunlit daytime
beaches (turquoise Caribbean water and white sand, Mediterranean cove, Pacific
coast with rolling waves, tropical palm-lined shore, bright beach dunes with sea
grass, clear blue lagoon). Strongly varied compositions and coastlines, luminous
color, realistic natural photography with fine detail, tasteful vacation photo
aesthetic. Only scenes: no people, no faces, no animals, no boats, no buildings,
no identifiable private locations, no text, no logos, no watermarks, no UI. Every
tile completely self-contained with no elements crossing the grid boundaries.
Output one cohesive contact-sheet asset at high resolution, ideally 2048 by 1152.
