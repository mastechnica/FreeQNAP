# Disk reports, SMART scheduling and notification rules

Storage & encryption offers reports for each identified internal SATA disk. Existing standby-safe inventory is saved hourly for 30 days, including SMART attributes and the device self-test log. Reports can be downloaded as JSON. History starts when the feature is installed; earlier samples are not reconstructed. USB enclosure member reports/tests remain unavailable and no vendor bridge commands are attempted.

Self-tests are opt-in per immutable serial: weekly short and monthly extended is the default preset, adjustable to 1–365 days. Enabling a schedule does not start a test immediately. The worker defers tests during RAID synchronization, degraded redundancy, standby/unavailable identity, or an already running self-test. It rechecks serial and SATA transport before requesting a fixed smartctl short/long test. It starts at most one test per worker pass. Successful command return is a test-start request, not a passed self-test: completion/results come from later SMART logs. No short/extended test has been physically validated by installing this feature; tests stay disabled until selected by the owner.

Disk alerts include overall SMART failure, nonzero reallocated/pending/uncorrectable sector counters, disk temperature at 50°C warning / 60°C critical, and failed latest self-tests when reported by the drive. Nonzero sector counters are warnings rather than claims of immediate failure. Missing readings never become a clean bill of health.

Health & notifications provides rules matching a condition category and warning/critical threshold. Rules choose email, push and/or buzzer. Presets cover critical-only, all problems/recoveries, storage protection, and dashboard-only. Multiple matching rules are additive. Local dashboard incidents always remain visible. Email/push still require separately configured and enabled transports; rule changes do not silently enable them or replay old events. Existing queued sends remain durable under their original destination/revision checks and retries. Suppressed new events are not automatically replayed when a rule is later enabled.

Buzzer output defaults off. Enable beeping explicitly, include Buzzer in a matching rule, and the active incident triggers at most one short MCU beep per minute. Silence indefinitely disables sound until re-enabled; timed mute accepts 1 minute to 7 days and survives service restarts. Mute does not acknowledge, resolve or hide faults, and does not change email/push. No unrequested audible or outbound test is sent by deployment. A beep ACK proves command acceptance, not audibility. Hardware/controller or total OS failure may prevent sound and local notification delivery.

Verification includes offline rule/category/channel selection, mute persistence and disabled sound, disk-report storage and RAID-busy scheduling, plus the existing durable notification retry tests. Actual scheduled-drive self-test completion, physical buzzer sound/mute, owner-configured email/push receipt and real remote VPN peers remain owner acceptance tests.
